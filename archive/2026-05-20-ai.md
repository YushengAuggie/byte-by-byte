### 1. 🎨 Figma 推出 AI 设计 Agent / Figma Launches AI Design Agent

Figma 发布了一款**产品设计 AI Agent**，可以直接在 Figma Design 中帮助生成、编辑设计项目，以及"自动化繁琐工作"。这是继 Canva 和 Adobe 之后，又一家创意工具公司推出 AI 助手。

Figma has launched a **product design AI agent**, initially available in Figma Design, that can assist with generating or editing design projects and "automate busywork." It follows similar moves by Canva and Adobe.

📌 **对工程师的意义：** 设计稿生成速度将大幅提升，前端工程师与设计师的协作方式可能发生变化——未来的 PRD 可能直接附带 AI 生成的交互原型。

**Why it matters for engineers:** Design iteration will speed up dramatically. The frontend dev↔designer handoff may shift — future specs might ship with AI-generated interactive prototypes.

🔗 [Figma Blog: The Figma Agent is Here](https://www.figma.com/blog/the-figma-agent-is-here/)

### 2. 🤖 Andrej Karpathy 加入 Anthropic / Andrej Karpathy Joins Anthropic

前特斯拉 AI 负责人、OpenAI 联合创始人 **Andrej Karpathy** 宣布加入 Anthropic，专注 R&D 工作。他此前一直在研究"AI-native 新型学校"的教育项目，称自己"对教育仍深度热情"，日后会回归教育方向。

Former Tesla AI head and OpenAI co-founder **Andrej Karpathy** announced he's joining Anthropic to work on R&D. He had been building an "AI-native school" and says he remains "deeply passionate about education" and plans to return to it "in time."

📌 **背景：** Karpathy 是深度学习领域最有影响力的工程师之一（3Blue1Brown 风格的教学视频、nanoGPT 作者）。他的加入意味着 Anthropic 在基础研究上的雄心。

**Context:** Karpathy is one of the most influential engineers in deep learning (nanoGPT, famous teaching videos). His joining signals Anthropic's serious research ambition.

🔗 [The Verge: Andrej Karpathy joins Anthropic](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence)

### 3. 🎵 Google Flow Music 获移动 App + 粒度编辑 / Google Flow Music Gets Mobile App & Granular Editing

Google 的 AI 音乐生成工具 **Flow Music** 已在 iOS 上线，Android 即将推出。新功能包括：
- 对 AI 生成歌曲的**局部编辑**（修改某段的节奏、改写某句歌词）
- 生成"翻唱版"和**音乐视频**

Google's AI music generation tool **Flow Music** is now available on iOS (Android coming soon). New features include granular editing tools for changing portions of an AI-generated song — beat drops, lyrics rewrites — plus the ability to generate "covers" and music videos.

📌 **趋势：** AI 媒体创作工具正从"生成一次"向"可迭代编辑"演进，这与代码 IDE 的发展路径高度类似。

**Trend:** AI creative tools are evolving from "one-shot generation" to "iterative editing" — the same trajectory as code IDEs.

🔗 [Google Blog: Flow Music Updates](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-labs/flow-updates/)

### 4. 💰 Google AI Ultra 降价 / Google AI Ultra Gets Price Cut

Google 的顶级 AI 订阅套餐 AI Ultra（此前 $249.99/月）现在起步价调整为 **$100/月**，另有 $200/月 选项（含 Project Genie 世界模型访问权限）。这与 OpenAI Pro 的 $100/$200 两档定价结构一致。

Google's top AI subscription AI Ultra (previously $249.99/month) now reportedly starts at **$100/month**, with a $200/month option including Project Genie world model access. This mirrors OpenAI Pro's $100/$200 tier structure.

📌 **竞争格局：** 两大 AI 巨头的高端套餐定价趋同，消费者对 AI 订阅价格的接受上限正在被 $100-$200/月 这个锚点固化。

**Competition:** The two biggest AI players converging on the same pricing signals a market anchor forming around the $100-200/month range for premium AI.

🔗 [The Verge: Google AI Ultra pricing](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence)
