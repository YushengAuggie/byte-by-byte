# 🗣️ 软技能 / Soft Skills — Day 43
**Topic:** Onboarding New Team Members
**Category:** Leadership | **Level:** Senior/Staff

---

## 为什么这个问题重要 / Why This Matters

面试官问 onboarding，考察的是你对**工程师生产力和团队文化**的系统性思考。一个 Senior/Staff 工程师能让新人30天达到独立产出，而普通工程师需要90天 — 这差距直接影响团队交付速度。

*Interviewers use this to gauge your systemic thinking about team productivity and culture. A great senior engineer cuts time-to-productivity from 90 days to 30. That's a 3x multiplier on new hire ROI.*

---

## STAR Breakdown

### Situation 情境
"When I joined Stripe as a senior engineer, I noticed new hires were taking 2-3 months to make their first meaningful contribution. The onboarding was just a Confluence doc dump — no structure, no mentorship pairing."

### Task 任务
"As I started managing ICs, I owned onboarding for 4 engineers over 18 months. I needed to get them productive faster without sacrificing code quality or culture."

### Action 行动
我建立了一套 **30-60-90 day onboarding framework**:

**Week 1: 环境 + 上下文 (Context)**
- Day 1: 环境搭建完全 scripted（一个命令跑通），不让新人浪费时间
- 配对导师 (buddy)，同 level，不是 manager
- 系统架构 tour：「我们的核心数据流是什么」，用 whiteboard，不用 slides

**Week 2-4: 带飞 (Shadowing + Small PRs)**
- 第一个 PR：bug fix，有明确边界，有完整测试
- Pair programming：我 drive，他们 navigate，然后反过来
- 每日 30min 1:1：blockers 优先

**Month 2: 独立飞 (Independence)**
- 给一个 medium-complexity story，全程独立完成，我 review
- 介绍跨团队 stakeholders
- 鼓励提问 + 更新文档（边学边改 docs）

### Result 结果
"All 4 engineers made meaningful code contributions by week 3, and 3 of them were leading features by month 2. One told me later that our onboarding was 'the best I've ever experienced.'"

---

## ❌ Bad vs ✅ Good

❌ **"I just shared the docs and told them to read first."**
→ 被动，没有 structure，没有 accountability

❌ **"I assigned them a big feature on day 1 to throw them in the deep end."**
→ 可能让新人 overwhelmed，尤其是 mid-level

✅ **"I used a 30-60-90 framework with deliberate ramp from small wins to independent ownership, with daily check-ins to unblock early."**

---

## Senior/Staff Tips

🎯 **要主动设计 onboarding，不要等 HR 做**
Senior 工程师的价值之一：让别人更快成功

🎯 **第一周的 WIN 很关键**
新人第一周搭好环境、合并第一个 PR，信心大增。"Setup hell" 是信心杀手。

🎯 **文档是 onboarding 的副产品**
让新人更新他们觉得不清楚的文档 — 既让他们贡献，又让文档保持 fresh

🎯 **Buddy ≠ Manager**
Buddy 应该是 peer，心理安全感更高，更容易问"蠢问题"

---

## Key Takeaways — 面试要点

1. **结构化 30-60-90 框架** > 随机 ad-hoc
2. **第一周小赢 → 建立信心** → 快速独立
3. **Buddy 制** → peer 支持，心理安全
4. **数据说话**：新人 time-to-first-PR、time-to-independent-story

## 🧒 ELI5
就像教小孩骑自行车：不是直接松手让他骑，而是先扶着跑，然后慢慢放手，最后他自己骑。好的 onboarding 就是让新人有"辅助轮"的那段时间，建立信心，再逐步独立。

*Teaching someone to ride a bike: you don't just push and let go. You run alongside, gradually let go, then watch them ride. Good onboarding gives the "training wheels" phase with deliberate transition to independence.*
