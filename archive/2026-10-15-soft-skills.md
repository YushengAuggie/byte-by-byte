# 🗣️ 软技能 / Soft Skills — Day 139 (Synthesis)
## 从个人贡献者到技术领导：Staff+ 的思维升级
## From IC to Tech Lead: The Staff+ Mindset Shift

> **综合题 / Synthesis Question:**
> "Walk me through how your definition of 'impact' has evolved over your engineering career. What does high-leverage work look like at your current level?"
>
> 「聊聊你对「影响力」的理解是如何随职业发展变化的。在你现在的级别，什么是高杠杆的工作？」

---

### 为什么这个问题很重要 / Why This Matters

这是 Staff+ 面试的核心问题之一。面试官在评估：
- 你的视野是否超越了个人产出
- 你是否理解组织目标和技术的连接
- 你是否能 multiply 别人的效率

This is a core Staff+ interview question. The interviewer evaluates:
- Whether your view extends beyond personal output
- Whether you connect org goals to technical decisions
- Whether you can multiply others' effectiveness

---

### ⭐ STAR 拆解

**Situation:** "Earlier in my career as a senior engineer, impact meant shipping features — velocity, code quality, uptime. I measured myself by what I personally built."

**Task:** "As I moved toward Staff, I kept seeing projects succeed technically but fail to move business metrics. I realized I was optimizing for the wrong thing."

**Action:** 三个具体转变 / Three concrete shifts:
1. **从执行者到放大器 / Executor → Multiplier** — 开始做代码审查培训、架构决策文档（ADR），让整个团队的工程质量提升，而不是只有自己写好代码
2. **从问题解决者到问题定义者 / Problem Solver → Problem Definer** — 在 PM 写需求之前，主动问「我们真正想解决的用户痛点是什么？」——避免了两次大型功能的重做
3. **从技术优先到影响优先 / Tech-first → Impact-first** — 学会说「这个技术很酷，但对 OKR 没有直接贡献，我们先做 X」

**Result:** "The team's shipping velocity actually increased 30% after I stopped trying to do everything myself. More importantly, I could point to 2 projects where early architecture input prevented months of rework."

---

### ❌ Bad vs ✅ Good

❌ **避免这样回答：** "I've always cared about quality and impact." (空话，无具体例子)

✅ **更好的结构：** 给出一个明确的「认知转折点」——具体是哪件事让你意识到需要改变思路，改变后发生了什么。

---

### 🎯 Senior vs Staff vs Principal 的影响力差异

```
Senior:    My code/feature → team's codebase
Staff:     My decisions/docs → multiple teams' velocity  
Principal: My org's architecture → company's roadmap
```

面试中被问到「高杠杆工作」，Staff 级别的答案应该包含：
- **技术系统** (不只是代码，还有文档、ADR、工具链)
- **人的系统** (培养，招聘，onboarding)
- **流程系统** (减少重复决策，建立护栏而不是规则)

---

### 🔑 Key Takeaways

1. **高杠杆 = 力量倍增器。** 花 5 小时写一篇好的架构文档，能节省团队 50 小时的反复讨论。
2. **推动 > 执行。** Staff+ 的核心是找到正确的问题，而不是更快地解决错误的问题。
3. **沉默的影响力也要说出来。** 你阻止了一个坏决策，也是贡献——要学会量化和表达"avoided impact"。

### 🧒 ELI5
初级工程师就像一个好厨师——做好每道菜。Staff 工程师就像主厨——不一定亲手做每道菜，但负责整个厨房出品的质量和速度。
A junior engineer is like a great cook — makes each dish well. A Staff engineer is like a head chef — doesn't personally cook everything, but is responsible for the whole kitchen's output quality and speed.
