🗣️ **软技能 / Soft Skills** — Tell me about a time you successfully navigated organizational change

**为什么这很重要？ / Why this matters:**
在快速发展的科技公司中，唯一不变的就是变化（重组、技术栈迁移、优先级调整）。高级/专家工程师（Senior/Staff）需要展现出在混乱中找到方向、稳定团队并推动落地的能力。
In fast-paced tech companies, the only constant is change (re-orgs, tech stack migrations, shifting priorities). Senior/Staff engineers must show they can find direction in chaos, stabilize the team, and drive execution.

**STAR 拆解 / STAR Breakdown:**
- **S (Situation):** 描述一个具体的组织变革（例如：公司决定从单体架构迁移到微服务，或者你的团队被合并到另一个部门）。/ Describe a specific organizational change (e.g., migrating from monolith to microservices, or your team merging with another).
- **T (Task):** 你的任务是什么？（不仅是写代码，还包括沟通、规划、缓解团队焦虑）。/ What was your task? (Not just coding, but communication, planning, mitigating team anxiety).
- **A (Action):** 你采取了什么行动？（主动沟通、制定过渡计划、建立反馈机制）。/ What actions did you take? (Proactive communication, creating a transition plan, setting up feedback loops).
- **R (Result):** 结果如何？（平稳过渡、系统上线、团队士气恢复）。/ What was the result? (Smooth transition, system launch, team morale restored).

**❌ 糟糕的回答 / Bad Answer:**
“公司突然要换框架，我只能跟着学，然后把代码重写了一遍。虽然很烦，但我还是完成了。”
"The company suddenly wanted to switch frameworks, so I had to learn it and rewrite the code. It was annoying, but I got it done."
*(被动接受，抱怨，没有展现领导力 / Passive acceptance, complaining, no leadership shown)*

**✅ 优秀的回答 / Good Answer:**
“当公司决定从 AWS 迁移到 GCP 时，团队很焦虑。我主动组织了技术分享会，梳理了迁移的风险点，并制定了分阶段的迁移计划。我还与运维团队建立了每日同步机制，确保问题及时解决。最终我们提前两周完成了迁移，并且零故障。”
"When the company decided to migrate from AWS to GCP, the team was anxious. I proactively organized tech sharing sessions, identified migration risks, and created a phased plan. I also set up daily syncs with Ops to resolve issues quickly. We finished two weeks early with zero downtime."
*(主动出击，稳定军心，跨团队协作 / Proactive, stabilizing the team, cross-team collaboration)*

**💡 Senior/Staff 提示 / Senior/Staff Tips:**
- **Focus on the "Why":** 解释你如何理解并传达变革背后的商业逻辑。/ Explain how you understood and communicated the business logic behind the change.
- **Manage Up and Down:** 你不仅要安抚下属，还要向上管理预期。/ You must manage expectations up and soothe anxiety down.

**🔑 核心收获 / Key Takeaways:**
拥抱变化，成为团队的“稳定器”，而不是“抱怨者”。
Embrace change and be the "stabilizer" for the team, not the "complainer."
