### 📰 Story 1: AI 模型价格战重塑市场格局
### AI Model Price War Reshapes the Market

各大 AI 服务商在 7 月掀起激烈价格竞争，包括 Google 新推出的 Gemini 3.6 Flash、Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite，以及 xAI 的 Grok 4.5、OpenAI 的 GPT-5.6 多个变体（据报道代号 Sol/Terra/Luna）。据报道，输出 Token 成本从此前的 $25–$50 / 1M tokens 骤降至 $4–$6，降幅逾 80%。

A fierce price war erupted among AI providers in July. Google launched Gemini 3.6 Flash, 3.5 Flash-Lite, and 3.5 Flash Cyber; xAI released Grok 4.5; OpenAI reportedly released GPT-5.6 variants. Output token costs reportedly dropped from $25–$50 to $4–$6 per 1M tokens — an over 80% reduction.

**对开发者的意义 / What it means for devs:**  
过去"AI 太贵，实验一下就好"的心态正在消失。许多原本因成本放弃的 production AI 功能，现在的成本已经可以接受。  
The "AI is too expensive for production" mindset is fading. Features once shelved for cost reasons are now economically viable.

### 📰 Story 2: EU AI Act 正式进入执法阶段
### EU AI Act Enters Enforcement Phase

2026 年 7 月 10 日，欧盟《人工智能法案》(AI Act) 正式进入执法阶段。法案以**风险分级**为核心：高风险 AI 系统（如医疗、执法、就业决策）需通过合规审查；所有 AI 聊天机器人须明确披露其 AI 身份。

On July 10, 2026, the EU AI Act officially entered its enforcement phase. Core principle: **risk-based tiers**. High-risk AI (medical, law enforcement, employment decisions) must pass compliance reviews. All AI chatbots must disclose they are AI.

**对工程师的实际影响 / Practical impact for engineers:**
- 在欧洲部署的 AI 系统需新增"AI 披露"UI
- 高风险场景需要可解释性日志和人工审核接口
- GDPR + AI Act 双重合规成为欧洲 AI 产品标配

In Europe: add AI disclosure UI, log model decisions for explainability, and build human-review hooks for high-risk flows. GDPR + AI Act compliance is now baseline.

### 📰 Story 3: MIT/Stanford 研究——自我纠错能力比模型规模更重要
### MIT/Stanford: Self-Correction Beats Raw Model Size

MIT 和 Stanford 研究人员发表研究指出：对于复杂数学和逻辑推理任务，模型在推理过程中**自我纠错的能力**比模型参数规模更关键。这一发现支持了"o 系列"和"思维链"推理模型的设计方向。

Researchers from MIT and Stanford found that a model's ability to **self-correct during reasoning** is a more critical factor for hard math/logic tasks than sheer model size. This validates the design direction of "thinking" models like the o-series and Chain-of-Thought variants.

**工程启示 / Engineering takeaway:**
- 在 Agent 系统中，给模型"反思"步骤可能比升级模型更有效
- 小模型 + 多次推理迭代 > 大模型 + 单次推理（某些场景）

In agent pipelines, adding a reflection/self-critique step may outperform upgrading to a larger model.
