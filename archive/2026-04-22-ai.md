🤖 **AI** — AI News Roundup

**1. Google 街景化 AI / Google is Street View-ifying AI**
Google 正在利用 AI 从航拍和卫星图像中提取洞察，或者将“充满想象力的场景锚定在现实世界中”。据报道，这可能对城市规划者非常有用，或者可以把宇宙飞船放在纽约地标前面。
Google is using new AI tools to unlock insights from aerial and satellite images or anchor "imaginative scenes in the real world." Reportedly, this could be highly useful for urban planners, or for putting spaceships in front of New York landmarks.
🔗 [Source: The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence)

**2. Anthropic 的 Mythos 模型发现 271 个 Firefox 漏洞 / Anthropic's Mythos model finds 271 bugs in Firefox**
Mozilla 首席技术官 Bobby Holley 表示，Anthropic 专注于网络安全的 AI 模型 Claude Mythos Preview 在 Firefox 150 中发现了 271 个漏洞，称其能力“丝毫不亚于”顶级安全研究人员。不过，Mozilla 也表示尚未发现“顶尖人类研究人员无法发现的漏洞”。
Anthropic's cybersecurity-focused AI model, Claude Mythos Preview, found 271 bugs in Firefox 150. Mozilla CTO Bobby Holley called it "every bit as capable" as top security researchers. Reassuringly, Mozilla hasn't "seen any bugs that couldn't have been found by an elite human researcher," either.
🔗 [Source: The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence)

**3. Google 创始人要求工程师使用内部 AI 编程代理 / Google founder mandates engineers use internal AI coding agents**
据报道，Google 联合创始人 Sergey Brin 在给 DeepMind 员工的备忘录中表示，“必须强制每位 Gemini 工程师使用内部代理来完成复杂的多步任务”。这似乎是为了在 AI 编程竞赛中追赶 Anthropic 的步伐。
According to reports, Google co-founder Sergey Brin said in a memo to DeepMind employees that "every Gemini engineer must be forced to use internal agents for complex, multistep tasks." This appears to be a move to catch up to Anthropic in the AI coding race.
🔗 [Source: The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence)
