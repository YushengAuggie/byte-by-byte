### 📰 Story 1: Anthropic 披露 80% 代码由 Claude 编写
**Anthropic Reveals 80% of Production Code Written by Claude**

Anthropic 披露，2026 年 5 月合并进生产代码库的代码中，据报道超过 80% 是由 Claude 本身编写的。这一披露被定性为对 AI 递归自我改进的警示——一个 AI 系统现在大量参与改进其继任者的开发工作。与此同时，Anthropic 据报道已申请 IPO，估值据报道接近 1 万亿美元。

Anthropic disclosed that reportedly over 80% of code merged into its production codebase in May 2026 was authored by Claude itself. The disclosure was framed as a caution about recursive self-improvement — an AI system now substantially participates in building its own successors. Anthropic has also reportedly filed for a landmark IPO, with valuations reportedly approaching $1 trillion.

🔗 Source: [AI Weekly for Leaders — June 8, 2026](https://aidrivensucess.substack.com/p/ai-weekly-for-leaders-june-8-2026)

**工程师视角:** 这意味着 AI-assisted coding 已经从"好用的工具"进化到"主要贡献者"。如果你还没在工作流里深度整合 AI coding assistant，现在已经是竞争劣势了。

**Engineer's take:** AI-assisted coding has evolved from "useful tool" to "primary contributor." If you haven't deeply integrated AI coding assistants into your workflow, it's already a competitive disadvantage.

---

### 📰 Story 2: Snap 裁员 1000 人，AI 生成 65%+ 代码
**Snap Lays Off 1,000, Citing AI-Driven Productivity**

Snap 宣布裁员约 1,000 人并关闭 300+ 个职位，理由是"AI 的快速进步使更小的团队能实现相同的产出"。据报道，AI 现在生成了 Snap 超过 65% 的新代码。

Snap announced layoffs of approximately 1,000 employees and closure of 300+ open roles, citing "rapid advancements in AI allowing smaller teams to achieve the same output." AI reportedly now generates over 65% of Snap's new code.

🔗 Source: [Axios — AI News June 8, 2026](https://www.axios.com/2026/06/08/ai-news-nvidia-cosmos-3-openai-sites-solara-rtx-spark)

**关键信号:** 这不只是 Snap 的问题。行业趋势很清楚：AI 正在压缩团队规模，但同时提高了留下的人的产出预期。

**Key signal:** This isn't just Snap. The industry trend is clear: AI is compressing team sizes while raising output expectations for those who remain.

---

### 📰 Story 3: Nvidia 发布 Cosmos 3 — 物理 AI 基础模型
**Nvidia Unveils Cosmos 3 — Foundation Model for Physical AI**

Nvidia 发布了 Cosmos 3，一个为物理 AI 设计的基础模型，用于训练能与物理世界交互的 AI 系统——例如控制工厂机器人。同时发布的还有 RTX Spark 处理器，为本地 AI 工作负载提供算力，已被微软 Surface Laptop Ultra 采用。

Nvidia unveiled Cosmos 3, a foundation model for physical AI designed to train systems that interact with the physical world — like controlling factory robots. Also announced: the RTX Spark processor for local AI workloads, already adopted by Microsoft's Surface Laptop Ultra.

🔗 Source: [Axios — AI News June 8, 2026](https://www.axios.com/2026/06/08/ai-news-nvidia-cosmos-3-openai-sites-solara-rtx-spark)

**为什么重要:** 物理 AI（具身智能）正在从实验室走向工厂。Cosmos 3 是这个方向的基础设施层——类似于 CV 领域的 ImageNet 时刻。

**Why it matters:** Physical AI (embodied intelligence) is moving from lab to factory floor. Cosmos 3 is the infrastructure layer for this shift — similar to the ImageNet moment for computer vision.

## 🧒 ELI5

这周 AI 世界发生了三件大事：1) Anthropic 的 AI 自己写了大部分自己公司的代码（有点像机器人在建造更好的机器人）；2) Snap 因为 AI 太能干而裁了很多员工；3) Nvidia 造了一个让机器人能理解物理世界的 AI。总结：AI 不只会聊天了，它在真实工作中已经超过了很多人。

Three big things happened in AI this week: 1) Anthropic's AI wrote most of its own company's code (like robots building better robots); 2) Snap laid off employees because AI got too productive; 3) Nvidia built an AI to help robots understand the physical world. Summary: AI isn't just for chatting — it's outperforming humans in real work.
