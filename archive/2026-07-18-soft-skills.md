## Synthesis Reflection: Senior+ Communication Patterns

After covering 60 behavioral questions, the meta-pattern for senior/staff engineers:

**The STAR+ Framework (STAR with Systemic Thinking):**
- **Situation:** Set scale context (millions of users, 10-team org, $X impact)
- **Task:** Clarify your specific role vs. the team's
- **Action:** Show trade-off reasoning, not just execution
- **Result:** Quantify, then reflect on what you'd do differently
- **+Systemic:** How did this change your team/org beyond the immediate outcome?

**Senior-level differentiation signals:**
1. You talk about *influencing without authority* (cross-team impact)
2. You show *proactive problem discovery* (not just firefighting)
3. You discuss *building systems* not just solving instances (runbook, postmortem process)
4. You mention *mentoring/multiplying* others as part of your impact
5. You're honest about failure — and what the failure taught the org

**Saturday reflection prompt:** Think of your most recent engineering decision. Can you narrate it using STAR+? What was the systemic change it drove?
