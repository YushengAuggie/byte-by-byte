### 📰 本周 AI 大事件 / This Week in AI

**1. Mistral Large 4 ("Le Chonk") 发布 — 万亿参数 MoE 模型**

Mistral AI 于 10 月 5 日发布旗舰模型 Mistral Large 4，代号"Le Chonk"。据报道该模型拥有 1 万亿参数，但得益于混合专家架构（MoE），每个 token 仅激活约 490 亿参数，支持多模态（文本 + 图像）输入。

Mistral AI released Mistral Large 4, nicknamed "Le Chonk," on October 5. The model reportedly has 1 trillion parameters, but its Mixture-of-Experts architecture activates only ~49B parameters per token. It supports multimodal (text + image) input and is described as their most capable model to date.

📌 来源: [llm-releases.com](https://www.llm-releases.com/) | [llmgateway.io/timeline](https://llmgateway.io/timeline)

**2. Google Gemini Agent — 有自己日历和邮箱的 AI 助手**

Google Cloud 于 10 月初宣布推出 Gemini Agent，一个设计为跨工作区应用（Google Workspace、Microsoft 365、Slack）工作的通用 Agent。据报道该 Agent 拥有自己的账户、电子邮件地址和日历，能维持持久记忆并协调子 Agent。

Google Cloud announced in early October the Gemini agent — a universal agent designed to work across workplace apps including Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, and Slack. It reportedly has its own account, email address, and calendar, can maintain persistent memory, and can coordinate specialized sub-agents.

📌 来源: [hellogrowth.ai/en/blog/google-ai-news-october-2026](https://hellogrowth.ai/en/blog/google-ai-news-october-2026)

---

**3. Anthropic Claude Haiku 5.5 — 首个支持 1M 上下文窗口的 Haiku**

Anthropic 于 10 月 6 日发布 Claude Haiku 5.5。据报道这是第一个 Haiku 系列模型支持 effort controls（可调节推理深度）和 100 万 token 上下文窗口。

Anthropic released Claude Haiku 5.5 on October 6. It's reportedly the first Haiku model with effort controls and a 1M token context window, making it a more powerful lightweight option.

📌 来源: [llm-stats.com/llm-updates](https://llm-stats.com/llm-updates)

### 💡 技术洞察 / Tech Insight

这周的新闻有个明显趋势：**Agent 化（Agentic AI）正在进入企业工作流**。

Google 的 Gemini Agent 有自己的邮箱，Mistral 的 MoE 让万亿参数模型经济可行，Anthropic 的 effort controls 让开发者能平衡速度和智能。

The trend this week: **Agentic AI entering enterprise workflows.** Google's agent gets its own calendar, Mistral makes trillion-parameter models economically viable via MoE, and Anthropic gives developers fine-grained control over reasoning depth.
