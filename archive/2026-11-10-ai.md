### 📰 本周重大新闻 / Major Stories This Week

**1. OpenAI 发布 GPT-6.1 Terra 与 GPT-6 Rosalind**

OpenAI 于 10 月 26 日推出 GPT-6.1 Terra，聚焦视觉推理、电脑操控和专业任务，已在 ChatGPT Work、Codex 及 API 中上线。10 月 30 日，面向生命科学研究者的 GPT-6 Rosalind Research 也随之发布。

OpenAI launched GPT-6.1 Terra on Oct 26, focusing on visual reasoning and computer use. GPT-6 Rosalind Research followed on Oct 30, targeting approved life-sciences researchers.

📎 Source: https://aiedgeinsights.com/ai-news-november-4-2026-major-ai-developments/

---

**2. Anthropic 申请 IPO，季度营收据报接近 170 亿美元**

Anthropic 正式提交上市文件，据报道季度营收接近 170 亿美元。同月，Claude 的 coding agent 功能获得了跨 session 的记忆能力，可在长期项目中保留学习内容。

Anthropic filed to go public, with quarterly revenue reportedly nearing $17 billion. Claude's coding agent also gained persistent memory across sessions.

📎 Source: https://www.ptechpartners.com/2026/11/03/october-2026-ai-news-roundup-revenue-vs-compute/

---

**3. AI 安全警告：Geoffrey Hinton 称可能"本不该有 AI"**

"AI 教父" Geoffrey Hinton 公开警告，AI 可能在未来一年内引发金融系统或电网大规模中断事件，并呼吁强制性的前沿模型测试与国际监管标准。

Geoffrey Hinton warned AI could trigger a large-scale system blackout within a year, calling for mandatory frontier model testing and intergovernmental standards.

📎 Source: https://www.theguardian.com/uk-news/2026/nov/03/ai-godfather-geoffrey-hinton-better-off-without-ai

### 工程师视角 / Engineer's Take

作为工程师，这三条新闻背后有一个共同主题：**能力和风险在同步加速**。GPT-6 和 Claude 的新功能意味着 AI agent 已经可以独立执行长期任务；而 Hinton 的警告提醒我们，这些 agent 的安全边界还远未清晰。

*As engineers, there's one thread connecting these three stories: capability and risk are accelerating in lockstep. GPT-6 and Claude's new features mean AI agents can now run long-horizon tasks autonomously; Hinton's warning reminds us the safety boundaries of these agents remain unclear.*

---

### 🧒 ELI5

AI 变得越来越聪明，赚的钱也越来越多，但管它的规则还没跟上。就像汽车速度越来越快，但红绿灯还没升级。

*AI is getting smarter and making more money, but the rules governing it haven't caught up — like cars getting faster while traffic lights stay the same.*
