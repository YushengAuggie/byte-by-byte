### 📰 Story 1: 白宫申请 90 亿美元购买 AI 芯片用于情报机构

**The White House is asking for $9 billion to buy AI chips for spy agencies.**

据报道，CIA 和 NSA 缺乏足够的算力来运行最新的 AI 模型，白宫已批准一项申请，拟购置 Nvidia Grace Blackwell 超级芯片并建设相关基础设施，总计据报道约 90 亿美元，但仍需国会批准。

The White House has reportedly approved a request for ~$9 billion to buy cutting-edge chips and build infrastructure for spy agencies. The CIA and NSA reportedly lack the computing capacity to run the latest AI models. Infrastructure would support Nvidia's Grace Blackwell superchip. Congress must still approve the funds.

**为什么重要 / Why It Matters:**
国家情报机构的 AI 军备竞赛正在变得和民间科技公司一样白热化。算力即国力——这一点在 2026 年已经不是隐喻，而是字面意思。

The AI arms race has reached intelligence agencies. Compute = national power is no longer a metaphor.

📎 Source: [The Verge, May 24 2026](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence) (referencing NYT reporting)

### 📰 Story 2: Anthropic 与五角大楼的冲突：AI 自主战争的边界

**Anthropic's fight with the Pentagon highlights risks of autonomous warfare.**

Anthropic 据报道与美国国防部在 Claude 用于自主战争决策的边界问题上产生了摩擦，引发了业界对 AI 武器化的讨论。报道指出，虽然这一争议吸引了公众注意，但真正令人担忧的是：真正的自主 AI 军事系统比公众认知的要近得多。

Anthropic reportedly clashed with the Pentagon over the limits of Claude's use in autonomous warfare decisions. The Verge notes that while the public dispute makes headlines, the more alarming story is how close truly autonomous AI military systems actually are — closer than most people realize.

**为什么重要 / Why It Matters:**
AI 安全的战场已经不只是聊天机器人的对齐问题——它延伸到了字面意义上的"战场"。Anthropic 在商业利益和道德边界之间走钢丝。

AI safety is no longer just about chatbot alignment — it now extends to literal warfare. This is the real test of whether AI labs can hold ethical lines under pressure.

📎 Source: [The Verge, May 26 2026](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence)

### 📰 Story 3: Anthropic Project Glasswing 更新：安全工具向用户开放

**Anthropic makes Claude Mythos Preview security tools more available.**

Anthropic 更新了 Project Glasswing（据报道是其内部红队/安全项目），包括技能工具、Claude 安全测试框架和威胁模型构建器，现在可向"符合资质"的客户开放申请。同时发布了一个开源漏洞披露仪表板。

Anthropic updated Project Glasswing, making security tools — including skills, a Claude harness, and a threat model builder — available to "qualifying" customers upon request. Anthropic also published a dashboard of open-source vulnerabilities discovered by Claude Mythos Preview.

**为什么重要 / Why It Matters:**
AI 公司开始把"安全能力"本身作为产品输出，而不只是内部自用。这反映了 AI 安全生态系统的逐渐成熟——把模型的攻击性理解能力转化为防御工具。

AI companies are beginning to productize their safety capabilities. This reflects a maturing AI security ecosystem: turning the model's adversarial understanding into defensive tools for others.

📎 Source: [Anthropic Project Glasswing Update](https://www.anthropic.com/research/glasswing-initial-update) (via The Verge, May 22 2026)

### 💡 本周主题 / This Week's Theme

三条新闻共同指向一个趋势：**AI 正在快速进入高风险领域**——情报、军事、安全。民间实验室（Anthropic、OpenAI）突然需要回答以前只有政府才需要回答的问题：算力谁控制？决策权给不给 AI？红线在哪里？

All three stories point to a trend: **AI is rapidly entering high-stakes domains** — intelligence, warfare, security. Civilian labs now need to answer questions previously reserved for governments: Who controls the compute? How much autonomy for AI? Where are the red lines?
