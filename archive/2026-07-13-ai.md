### 📰 Story 1: OpenAI 发布 GPT-5.6 — Sol / Terra / Luna 三档模型
### OpenAI Releases GPT-5.6 in Three Tiers: Sol, Terra, Luna

**来源 / Source:** [SpaceDaily](https://spacedaily.com/m-on-july-9-2026-openai-opened-its-most-powerful-system-yet-to-the-public-gpt-5-6-in-three-tiers-named-sol-terra-and-luna-alongside-an-agent-it-calls-chatgpt-work-built-to-carry/) | [AI Intelligence Briefing](https://medium.com/@diwakar-dayalan/ai-intelligence-briefing-week-of-july-6-2026-5896a1416a2c)

OpenAI 于 **7 月 9 日**公开发布了迄今最强的系统 **GPT-5.6**，分为三档：

- **Sol** — 旗舰模型，面向前沿推理和长周期 agentic 任务；带 **"ultra mode"**，把复杂任务分解到并行子代理（subagents）执行。
- **Terra** — 性能接近 GPT-5.5，但成本约为其一半。
- **Luna** — 最便宜最快，面向高吞吐、低延迟场景。

同时发布 **ChatGPT Work** —— 一个"做完整份工作"而非仅回答问题的 agent。

*On July 9, OpenAI launched GPT-5.6 in three tiers — Sol (frontier reasoning + long-horizon agentic work, with an "ultra mode" that decomposes tasks across parallel subagents), Terra (near GPT-5.5 performance at ~half the cost), and Luna (cheapest/fastest for high-volume, low-latency). Alongside it: ChatGPT Work, an agent built to carry out entire jobs.*

**对工程师的意义 / Engineering Takeaway:**
```
GPT-5.6 Sol "ultra mode":
  复杂任务 ──▶ 分解 ──▶ [subagent 1] [subagent 2] [subagent 3] (并行)
                              │           │           │
                              └─── 汇总 / merge results ───┘
```
"ultra mode" 的并行子代理架构，正是我们在系统设计里讲的 **fan-out / scatter-gather** 模式落到 LLM 推理层——把一个大任务拆成可并行的子任务再聚合，用延迟换质量。

*The subagent fan-out is the classic scatter-gather pattern applied to LLM inference — decompose, run in parallel, merge. Trade latency for answer quality.*

### 📰 Story 2: Anthropic 年化营收突破 $30B，反超 OpenAI
### Anthropic's Annualized Revenue Crosses $30B, Overtaking OpenAI

**来源 / Source:** [AI Central](https://aicentral.substack.com/p/the-ai-landscape-july-2026) | [ReleaseBot: Anthropic](https://releasebot.io/updates/anthropic)

Anthropic 年化营收本月**突破 $300 亿**，比 OpenAI 高出近 $60 亿——这一反转归功于其**企业市场**的专注：采购合同、API 扩张、以及为企业工作流训练的 Claude。

产品侧：
- **Claude Sonnet 5** 在 Microsoft Foundry (Azure) 全面可用，企业可直接用已有的 Azure 消费额度部署。
- **Claude Cowork** 扩展到 web 和移动端，跨设备同步会话与文件，新增 Microsoft 365 写入工具（起草邮件、管理日历、创建/更新 OneDrive & SharePoint 文件）。
- **Claude Code** 新增内置桌面浏览器、改进的 `/doctor` 检查、以及 auto mode 安全增强。

*Anthropic's annualized revenue crossed $30B, roughly $6B ahead of OpenAI, driven by its enterprise focus. Claude Sonnet 5 went GA on Microsoft Foundry (Azure); Claude Cowork expanded to web/mobile with Microsoft 365 write tools; Claude Code added a built-in desktop browser, improved `/doctor`, and auto-mode safety upgrades.*

**对工程师的意义 / Engineering Takeaway:**
企业采购 + 云市场分发（Azure/Bedrock/Vertex）正在成为 AI 收入的主战场。"模型好"已不是壁垒，**部署摩擦最小化**（用现有云额度、审计日志、admin 控制）才是。

*The moat is shifting from raw model quality to deployment friction — existing cloud commits, audit logs, admin controls. Distribution beats benchmarks.*

### 📰 Story 3: Google Gemini 3.5 Pro 延期发布至 7 月
### Google's Gemini 3.5 Pro Slips to July

**来源 / Source:** [AI Landscape July 2026](https://aicentral.substack.com/p/the-ai-landscape-july-2026)

原定 6 月发布的 **Gemini 3.5 Pro** 推迟到 **7 月**——企业测试者在**推理和代码生成**性能上发现了问题，Google 选择推迟而非硬发。

与此同时，Google 正与 OpenAI、Anthropic 就一个**自愿框架**展开谈判：面向美国政府的前沿模型发布与早期安全测试流程（建立在 6 月的行政令基础上）。

*Gemini 3.5 Pro, promised for June, slipped to July after enterprise testers flagged reasoning and code-generation issues. Meanwhile Google, OpenAI, and Anthropic are negotiating a voluntary framework for releasing frontier models to the U.S. government with early security testing.*

**对工程师的意义 / Engineering Takeaway:**
"宁可延期，不发次品"——在 code-gen 这类高可验证任务上，回归（regression）藏不住。这也是为什么**评测门禁（eval gates）** 现在是模型发布流水线的一等公民，跟我们给 byte-by-byte 加的 QA gate 是同一个道理。

*"Delay over ship-broken" — regressions in verifiable tasks like code-gen can't hide. Eval gates are now first-class in release pipelines, same principle as our content QA gate.*

## 📌 本周主线 / This Week's Throughline

三家巨头，三种打法：
- **OpenAI** 冲能力上限 + agent 化（Sol ultra mode, ChatGPT Work）
- **Anthropic** 闷声赚企业钱（$30B 营收，云市场分发）

*Three giants, three strategies: OpenAI pushes capability + agents, Anthropic quietly wins enterprise, Google plays it safe. For interviews: don't recite model names — discuss architecture patterns, business logic, and engineering discipline. That's the senior/staff lens.*
