### 1. 🤝 Microsoft-OpenAI 协议大变：独家终结

据报道，微软与 OpenAI 修订了合作协议，**终止了云端独家性**。这意味着 OpenAI 现在可以通过亚马逊（AWS）和谷歌（GCP）分发其模型，而不再只绑定 Azure。

Microsoft and OpenAI have reportedly revised their partnership to **end exclusivity**, allowing OpenAI to distribute models through AWS and Google Cloud — not just Azure.

**为什么重要 / Why It Matters:**
- OpenAI 从"微软的 AI 部门"变成了真正独立的平台公司
- 对 Azure 短期有影响，但微软仍持有 OpenAI 大量股份
- 对开发者：OpenAI API 可能出现在更多云平台的 marketplace

### 2. 🤖 AI Agent 基础设施爆发：Stripe + Cloudflare 给 AI 刷卡

Stripe 推出了 **Link — AI Agent 数字钱包**，让 AI agent 可以自主完成支付。Cloudflare 和 Stripe 还联手推出了一个协议，据报道可以让 AI agent **自主部署应用、创建账户、购买域名**。

Stripe launched **Link for AI agents** — a digital wallet letting agents transact autonomously. Cloudflare + Stripe also launched a protocol reportedly enabling agents to deploy apps, create accounts, and purchase domains without human approval.

**为什么重要 / Why It Matters:**
- AI agent 从"能思考"进化到"能花钱办事"
- Agentic Commerce（AI 自主商务）从概念变成基础设施
- 对工程师：agent 授权和资金限制成为新的安全课题

📎 Source: [AI Magazine Top 5 Stories May 2, 2026](https://aimagazine.com/news/top-five-stories-in-ai-may-2-2026)

### 3. 📉 "SaaS 末日"是虚惊一场？Goldman Sachs 说别慌

高盛 CEO David Solomon 表示：市场对"AI 将摧毁 SaaS"的担忧**过度悲观**。Figma、Atlassian 等公司数据显示，AI 正在被**集成进**产品，用户量并没有因 AI 替代而下降。

Goldman Sachs CEO David Solomon says the "Saaspocalypse" fear — that AI would wipe out SaaS — was reportedly overdone. Companies like Figma and Atlassian show continued growth, suggesting AI is being integrated into tools, not replacing them.

**为什么重要 / Why It Matters:**
- SaaS 公司不是被 AI 取代，而是被迫成为"AI-native"平台
- 工程师价值在于构建 AI 集成能力，而非被替代
- 对求职者：展示你懂 AI 集成 > 担心 AI 抢工作

📎 Source: [Motley Fool — Goldman Sachs on AI SaaS, May 3, 2026](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/05/03/goldman-sachs-says-the-artificial-intelligence-ai/)

## 🧒 ELI5

以前的 AI 就像一个超聪明的顾问——给建议，但不能自己动手。现在的 AI agent 不只是给建议，还能自己开账户、买东西、部署代码。就像从"有个天才朋友"升级到"有个天才朋友，而且他有你的信用卡"。

Before, AI was like a super-smart advisor — gives advice, can't act. Now AI agents can open accounts, make purchases, and deploy code. It's like upgrading from "brilliant advisor" to "brilliant advisor who also has your credit card."
