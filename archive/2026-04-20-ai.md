## 📰 本周 AI 要闻 / This Week in AI

### 1. Sergey Brin：Google 要向 Anthropic 的编码智能体学习

据报道，Google 联合创始人 Sergey Brin 在内部备忘录中写道，Google 工程师必须强制使用内部 AI 智能体完成复杂任务，因为 Anthropic 的编码工具目前领先。他还暗示，赶上 Anthropic 是迈向"AI 自我改进"的一步。

According to reports, Google co-founder Sergey Brin wrote in an internal memo that every Gemini engineer "must be forced to use internal agents for complex, multistep tasks." Brin reportedly said Anthropic's coding tools are currently leading the race, and catching up is a step toward building AI that can improve itself.

🔗 [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence) · [The Information原报道](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/google-creates-strike-team-improve-coding-models)

### 2. Anthropic 发布 Claude Design（Opus 4.7 驱动）

Anthropic 推出 Claude Design，基于最新的 Opus 4.7 模型，允许用户创建设计稿、原型、Pitch Deck 和营销素材。目前在付费用户中以研究预览版开放。

Anthropic launched Claude Design — powered by Claude Opus 4.7 — letting users create designs, prototypes, pitch decks, and marketing materials. Available in research preview for paying subscribers.

🔗 [The Verge 报道](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/913184/anthropic-claude-opus-4-7-cybersecurity)

### 3. AI 影响力账号涌入社交媒体

据纽约时报调查，Instagram、TikTok、Facebook 上出现了数百个 AI 生成的假账号，疑似服务于政治目的。随着 AI 生成头像的成本大幅降低，批量创建虚假影响力账号变得前所未有的容易。

The New York Times found hundreds of AI-generated fake accounts across Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook, reportedly pushing political messaging. Experts note that creating such avatars is increasingly cheap and easy, with entire contractor networks specializing in bulk AI avatar deployment.

🔗 [The Verge 报道](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence)
