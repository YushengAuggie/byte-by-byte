## 📰 本周大事 / This Week's Top Stories

### 1. 🤖 Claude 证明87年数学猜想
Anthropic 的 Claude Fable 5 模型据报道发现了 Jacobian 猜想（一个87年未解的数学难题）的反例，引发数学界震动。

Anthropic's Claude Fable 5 reportedly discovered a counterexample to the Jacobian conjecture — an 87-year-old unsolved math problem — sending shockwaves through the math community.

📎 Source: https://www.aiapps.com/blog/ai-news-august-breakthroughs-launches-trends-cant-miss/

### 2. 💰 GPT-5.6 价格暴降 80%
OpenAI 将其 GPT-5.6 Luna 模型价格削减 80%，降至每百万 token 仅 $0.20，标志着 LLM 进入"大宗商品化"阶段。谷歌同步跟进价格战。

OpenAI slashed GPT-5.6 Luna pricing by 80% to $0.20/1M input tokens. Google followed suit. LLMs are becoming commodities — the real competition is now on context, latency, and integration.

📎 Source: https://buttondown.com/agent-k/archive/llm-daily-august-05-2026/

---

### 3. 🌍 EU AI Act 透明度义务正式生效
2026 年 8 月 2 日，《欧盟 AI 法案》透明度条款正式实施。凡向欧盟市场提供 AI 系统的全球厂商，均须满足信息披露要求，违者面临巨额罚款。

The EU AI Act's transparency obligations took effect August 2, 2026. Any AI provider placing systems on the EU market or whose AI outputs are used in the EU must comply — globally. Non-compliance triggers significant fines.

📎 Source: https://www.cooley.com/news/insight/2026/2026-08-03-eu-ai-act-transparency-obligations-take-effect-2-august-2026

### 4. 🤖 AI Agent 全面落地
- **Google**: 将 Gemini 集成进 Android，取代 Google Assistant；推出 Gemini Spark（云端常驻 agent，据报道 $99.99/月）
- **Anthropic**: 推出 Claude Cowork，桌面端 multi-step agent，据报道 $20/月
- **ChatGPT**: 据报道周活跃用户约 10 亿

Agents are no longer demos — they're shipping products. The race is now about **distribution + reliability**, not just raw capability.

📎 Source: https://www.aiapps.com/blog/ai-news-august-breakthroughs-launches-trends-cant-miss/

## 🔍 工程师视角 / Engineer's Take

这周的新闻有一个共同主题：**AI 正在脱离"实验室玩具"阶段**。

1. **价格战** → API 成本下降对开发者是利好，但对 AI 公司的商业模式是压力
2. **Agent 商品化** → 真正的护城河在于数据、分发渠道、垂直领域集成
3. **监管落地** → EU AI Act 会推动整个行业向透明度和可解释性投资
4. **数学突破** → AI 辅助科研的速度在加快，不只是写代码

The through-line: AI is commoditizing fast. The question for engineers: **what layer do you build on**, and **how do you add value that's not easily replicable by the next model version**?
