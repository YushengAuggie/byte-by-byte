## Quick Reference

**gRPC vs REST:**
- gRPC: binary (protobuf), HTTP/2, strongly typed, supports streaming
- REST: text (JSON), HTTP/1.1, flexible, universal tooling

**When to use gRPC:** internal microservice communication, high-throughput, streaming needs (server-side, client-side, bidirectional).
